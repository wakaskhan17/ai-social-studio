Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 October 2026

This policy describes the intended handling of personal data by AI Social Studio, a desktop social-media content application in development. It will be reviewed before any public launch or live TikTok integration.

1. Information processed

The current local prototype stores post instructions, captions, hashtags, scheduling information and related draft metadata locally on the user's device. If TikTok authorization is enabled in a later version, the application may also process TikTok account identifiers, access tokens, authorized profile information and publishing status needed to provide requested features.

2. Why information is used

Information is used to prepare and manage posts, perform functions requested by the user, authenticate authorized accounts and diagnose operational errors.

3. Storage and security

The current draft data is stored locally. Future TikTok credentials should be secured with appropriate protections and must not be exposed in public repositories, logs or webpages. No TikTok connection or public publishing functionality is enabled in the current prototype.

4. Sharing

If integration is enabled, information required for a user-authorized post will be transmitted to TikTok and any other explicitly integrated provider. Such processing is governed by the relevant provider's policies. We do not sell users' personal information.

5. Retention and deletion

Locally saved draft data can be deleted from the application's local storage. Once account connection features are implemented, users will be able to request disconnection and deletion of associated credentials. Content already posted on TikTok must be managed according to TikTok's available controls.

6. Your choices

You may decline or revoke TikTok authorization through its settings. For access, correction or deletion enquiries, use the contact address below.

7. Updates

We will update this page to reflect changes to functionality, external hosting, analytics, service providers or retention practices before those features are launched.
